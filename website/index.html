The problem

Is it a pulled muscle, or a heart attack?

Chest pain at home or in the office is confusing. A strained muscle, acidity and a heart attack can feel alike. Many people wait and hope it passes, and lose the time that matters most.

Muscle pain

Heart signals look normal

Steady ECG, heart attack markers low. The app tells you your heart signals look normal, and to keep an eye on how you feel.

or
Heart attack warning

Heart signals are changing

ECG shows heart attack signs, markers rising. You get an urgent alert on your phone, with one-tap SOS for the nearest ambulance.

If your pain is severe or you're unsure, always call for emergency help. CardiosensAI does not replace a doctor.

How it works

Two devices. One app on your phone.

The ECG monitor and the biomarker patch work separately. Each sends its results to the CardiosensAI app on your phone. Alerts come to you first, so you can act early.

Device 1

Records the heart's electrical signal, beat by beat, day and night.

Our AI

What our AI spots in an ECG

Our model is trained to find many kinds of heart problems in ECG data, including:

  • Irregular rhythm (AFib)
  • Heartbeat too fast or too slow
  • Extra beats (PVCs, PACs)
  • Bundle branch blocks (RBBB, LBBB)
  • Signs of a heart attack
  • Many other abnormalities

The app does not replace a doctor. It tells you when you should get checked.
